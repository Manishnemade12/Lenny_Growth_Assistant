# Product-Market Fit: Lessons from Lenny's Podcast

## Overview
Finding Product-Market Fit (PMF) is the primary goal of any early-stage startup. In this episode, experts discuss how to evaluate and accelerate PMF.

## Key Insights
- **Rahul Vohra's Engine**: Ask users how disappointed they would be without your product. If >40% say "very disappointed", you have PMF.
- **Retention Curves**: PMF manifests as a flattening retention curve. If the curve hits zero, you do not have PMF.
- **Growth Loops**: Sustainable growth comes from compounding product loops rather than one-off acquisition tactics.
